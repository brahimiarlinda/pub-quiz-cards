Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who is responsible

Arlinda Brahimi
Email: brahimi.arlinda@gmail.com

Pub Quiz Cards is a free, non-commercial hobby project.

2. Using the app without an account

You can use every feature without signing in. Your progress (“Got it” / “Missed”), quizzes, teams and settings are saved only in your own browser (local storage) on your device. They are not sent to us. The app sets no cookies.

3. Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open the site, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page and keep the service secure. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in providing the website).

4. Visitor statistics

We use GoatCounter to count visits. It does not use cookies and does not store your IP address. It records the page visited, the referring website, browser and operating system, screen size and an approximate country. We also count which features are used (for example “host mode started”), without any link to you. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in understanding how the app is used).

5. Optional account

If you choose to sign in, we use Supabase (Supabase, Inc.) to manage your login and store your data. Your data is stored on servers in Frankfurt, Germany.

  • What we store: your email address, the time you signed up and last signed in, and your quiz data (progress, saved quiz, teams and the questions you have already used).
  • Why: so you can sign in with an emailed link and use your data on all your devices.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the account you asked for).
  • How long: until you delete it. “Delete my saved data” in the account menu removes your quiz data immediately. To delete your account and email address completely, email us and we will do it within 30 days.
  • Supabase acts as our processor under a data processing agreement. Where data could be accessed from outside the EU, this is covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • When sign-in is available, the app loads the Supabase software library from the jsDelivr network, which receives your IP address to deliver the file.

6. Reporting a mistake

The “Report a mistake” link opens a page on GitHub. Anything you post there is public and handled by GitHub under its own privacy statement.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and export your data, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. Contact us at the email address above. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Saxon Data Protection and Transparency Commissioner (Sächsische Datenschutz- und Transparenzbeauftragte).
